# Methodology Update: Palestine Proper vs. Southern Levant

## Rationale

The project will now maintain two separate geographic accounting frameworks.

### 1. Palestine Proper

This is the narrow historical-demographic unit. It includes the districts and towns normally treated as Ottoman Palestine and corresponding broadly to present-day Israel/Palestine.

Use this scope for claims such as:

- "X refugees settled in Palestine proper."
- "Y immigrants entered districts later corresponding to Israel/Palestine."
- "Z population growth occurred in Jaffa, Haifa, Jerusalem, Nablus, Hebron, Gaza, Acre, Safed, or Tiberias."

### 2. Southern Levant

This is a broader Ottoman-era mobility zone. It includes Palestine proper plus adjacent regions such as Transjordan, Hauran, Golan, Balqa, Amman, Salt, Wadi Sir, Jarash, and other directly relevant borderlands.

Use this scope for claims such as:

- "X refugees were settled in the Southern Levant."
- "Ottoman settlement programs placed refugees in regions adjacent to Palestine."
- "These settlements created potential secondary migration channels into Palestine."

## Evidence Rule

Do not count Southern Levant settlement as Palestine immigration unless one of the following exists:

1. Direct record of relocation into Palestine proper.
2. Population register or census data showing birthplace/origin from Southern Levant.
3. Land, marriage, tax, military, or appointment record showing movement into Palestine.
4. A clearly documented chain of secondary migration.

## Paper Language

Preferred formulation:

"Rather than treating modern political borders as self-evident units of analysis, this study distinguishes Palestine proper from a broader Southern Levant mobility zone. This allows refugee settlement in Transjordan, Hauran, and the Golan to be analyzed as historically relevant to Palestine without automatically counting those populations as immigrants into Palestine."

## Practical Database Rule

Every event, settlement, population figure, and residual estimate should be tagged as one of:

- PAL_PROPER
- SOUTHERN_LEVANT
- BROADER_OTTOMAN

When uncertain, use the narrowest defensible scope and mark the confidence as Low or Medium.
