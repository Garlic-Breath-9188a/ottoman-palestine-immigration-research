# Research Protocol

## Research Question

What role did immigration and refugee settlement play in the population growth of Ottoman Palestine between 1830 and 1914?

## Geographic Scope

Primary scope:

- Jerusalem Sanjak
- Acre/Akka Sanjak
- Nablus Sanjak where relevant
- Districts corresponding broadly to present-day Israel/Palestine

Do not conflate Palestine proper with:

- Transjordan
- Hauran
- Golan
- Damascus Vilayet
- Lebanon

These areas may be included only as comparative or neighboring settlement zones.

## Evidence Classes

### Class A: Primary Ottoman Records

Examples:

- Muhacirin Commission records
- Ottoman population registers
- Salnames
- Ottoman census tables
- Land grant records
- Port records

### Class B: Contemporary Non-Ottoman Sources

Examples:

- British consular reports
- French consular reports
- Missionary reports
- Travel accounts

### Class C: Modern Academic Scholarship

Examples:

- Kemal Karpat
- Justin McCarthy
- Hamed-Troyansky
- Gad Gilbar
- Alexander Schölch
- Yehoshua Ben-Arieh
- Ruth Kark

### Class D: Demographic Inference

Examples:

- Residual growth calculations
- Estimated natural increase
- Back-calculated net migration

## Confidence Scoring

- High: direct primary source with clear count, date, and location
- Medium: contemporary or academic source with clear basis but not primary
- Low: inferred, estimated, disputed, or missing full citation
- Speculative: hypothesis only, not evidence

## Data Rules

Every figure must include:

- Source
- Page or archive reference, where available
- Location
- Date or date range
- Population group
- Whether the figure is direct or inferred
- Confidence level
