# Notes: Frantzman Origin Tables Coding

## Key Analytical Point

Table 2.8 and Table 3.6 should not be treated as final evidence of external immigration volume. They are coding maps. Their value is that they identify the locations and settlement categories where external migration may be hiding.

## Strong External-Origin Leads

- Foreigners category in Table 2.8: Algerians, Egyptians, Circassians, Bosnians.
- Kh. Hawsa: Algerians.
- Huseiniya: Algerian Bedouin / Arab Zubaydat.
- Nebi Yusha: Lebanese.
- Hamma: linked to Mukheiba in Transjordan in the surrounding discussion.
- Muftahira: possibly Egyptian according to surrounding discussion.

## Ambiguous but Important Categories

- Abdul Hamid II settlers.
- Effendi and settlers.
- Farm settlements.
- Bedouin tribes.

## Risk

Do not count all Bedouin settlement as external migration. Some tribes had long histories within Palestine. The correct question is whether specific tribal ranges crossed Palestine / Transjordan / Hauran / Golan boundaries or whether settlement fixation reflected movement from outside Palestine proper.
