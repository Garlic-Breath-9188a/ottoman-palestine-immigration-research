# Coding Rules: External Migration Lens

## Purpose

These rules are designed to prevent the paper from accepting low-migration conclusions too quickly when source categories are ambiguous.

## Origin Coding

Each settler origin should be coded as one of the following:

### ORIGIN_LOCAL_SUBDISTRICT

Settlers came from a nearby village, same nahiya, same kaza, or clearly local parent settlement.

### ORIGIN_PALESTINE_PROPER

Settlers came from elsewhere inside Palestine proper, but not the same local district.

### ORIGIN_SOUTHERN_LEVANT

Settlers came from Transjordan, Hauran, Golan, Balqa, Amman, Salt, Wadi Sir, Jarash, southern Syria, or adjacent Lebanon.

### ORIGIN_EXTERNAL_OTTOMAN

Settlers came from another Ottoman province outside the Southern Levant, such as Egypt, Anatolia, the Balkans, or North Africa.

### ORIGIN_REFUGEE_NONARAB_MUSLIM

Settlers were Circassian, Chechen, Dagestani, Bosnian, Albanian, or other Muslim refugees.

### ORIGIN_BEDOUIN_MOBILE

Settlers were Bedouin or semi-nomadic groups whose pre-settlement range may cross district or regional boundaries.

### ORIGIN_UNKNOWN

Origin is not clear enough to code.

## Analytical Rule

Do not classify an origin as "non-external" merely because the source says "local" unless the source identifies the actual origin locality.

## Reporting Rule

Totals must be reported in three separate columns:

1. Palestine Proper direct external migration
2. Southern Levant regional migration pool
3. Possible or documented secondary migration into Palestine

## Treatment of Frantzman

Frantzman is a settlement inventory, not the final authority on immigration scale. His tables should be re-coded under this framework.
