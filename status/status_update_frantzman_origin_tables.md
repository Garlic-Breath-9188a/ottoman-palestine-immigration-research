# Status Update: Frantzman Origin Tables Extracted

## Tables Extracted

### Table 2.8: Origins of settlers of new Arab settlements, 1871-1922

Frantzman classifies 69 new settlements as follows:

- Daughter Village: 19
- Bedouins: 9.5
- Foreigners, including Algerians, Egyptians, Circassians and Bosnians / Abdul Hamid II's settlers: 8.5
- Abdul Hamid II's settlers: 8
- Farm: 4
- City dwellers, e.g. Gazans: 4
- Effendi and settlers: 5
- Previous villagers: 2
- Locals: 2
- Unknown: 4

## Immediate Significance

This table materially weakens any blanket claim that external migration was insignificant because Frantzman himself gives an explicit "Foreigners" category of 8.5 out of 69 pre-1922 settlements, plus an ambiguous but important Abdul Hamid II settler category of 8.

The key move is not to inflate the numbers. It is to show that Frantzman's own settlement inventory contains external-origin and state-sponsored categories that require deeper extraction.

## Table 3.6: Origins of settlers of new villages, 1922-1931

The table provides village-level origin coding for 52 settlements. The dominant pattern is Bedouin settlement, but there are also explicit Southern Levant and external-origin leads:

- Kh. Hawsa: Algerians
- Huseiniya: Algerian Bedouin / Arab Zubaydat
- Nebi Yusha: Lebanese
- Hamma: Arab settlers; railroad, Abdul Hamid II, and effendi investment; later ownership by people from Mukheiba in Transjordan
- Muftakhira and Barjifyat: unknown but nearby discussion says Muftahira may have been populated by Egyptians
- Multiple Bedouin tribal settlements with potentially cross-border or Southern Levant mobility histories

## Research Implication

Frantzman's conclusion that "very few" settlers came from outside Mandatory Palestine is now testable against his own tables. His tables show that external-origin categories exist. The remaining task is to convert these from settlement counts into population estimates and origin-coded migration categories.
