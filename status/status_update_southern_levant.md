# Status Update: Southern Levant Geographic Scope

## Update

The project will now track two core geographic units:

1. Palestine Proper
2. Southern Levant

This reflects the fact that Ottoman administrative, transportation, and settlement practices did not correspond neatly to modern borders.

## Why This Matters

Syria, Hauran, Golan, and Transjordan bordered Palestine and were connected through Ottoman administrative structures, trade routes, military service, tribal movement, and refugee settlement programs. Refugees settled in these adjacent regions may have later moved into Palestine proper or contributed indirectly to demographic change there.

## Research Discipline

Southern Levant settlement will not be automatically counted as Palestine immigration.

Instead, the project will track:

- refugees settled in Palestine proper
- refugees settled in adjacent Southern Levant regions
- documented secondary migration into Palestine
- possible demographic residual effects

## New Research Question

How many Muslim refugees entered the Southern Levant under Ottoman settlement programs, and what evidence exists that these populations contributed to demographic growth in Palestine proper?
