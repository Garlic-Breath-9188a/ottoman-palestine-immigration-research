# Status Update: External Migration Argument Lens

## New Direction

The paper will be structured to substantially weaken the claim that external migration into Ottoman/Mandatory Palestine was insignificant.

This does not mean asserting an unsupported high number. It means showing that the low-migration interpretation depends on narrow categories and may systematically miss several types of migration.

## Evidence Found

Frantzman's dissertation states that understanding new village formation requires understanding where settlers came from and acknowledges that Arab and Muslim immigration into Palestine is a contentious question.

He also states that many new-village origins do not indicate outside-Palestine origins except for:

1. migrating Bedouin tribes
2. two unique villages established by Muslim refugees

This is useful because those exceptions are precisely the categories the paper needs to isolate and expand.

## Argument Shift

Instead of asking only:

"How many immigrants entered Palestine proper?"

the paper now asks:

"How much external migration is hidden by modern borders, ambiguous local-origin categories, failed settlements, assimilated migrants, Bedouin movement, and urban labor flows?"
